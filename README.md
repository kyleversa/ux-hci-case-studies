# UX & HCI Case Studies

A collection of UX and Human-Computer Interaction case studies focused on usability testing, heuristic evaluation, product analysis, and design recommendations.

These projects combine structured research methods with product-focused analysis to evaluate real digital experiences and identify opportunities for improvement.

## Case Studies

### Starbucks Mobile App Usability Test

A task-based usability evaluation of the Starbucks iOS mobile app focused on drink customization, Starbucks Card reload, and store locator flows.

**Methods used:** usability testing, think-aloud observation, task completion analysis, time-on-task measurement, error tracking, qualitative observation, System Usability Scale (SUS), severity ranking, and evidence triangulation.

**Key outcome:** The app received an average SUS score of **64.5**, slightly below the common benchmark of **68**. The most significant usability friction appeared in customization visibility and reload/payment confidence.

**Main recommendations:**

- Make customization options more visible.
- Clarify the reload/payment flow.
- Improve store locator clarity.
- Reduce visual clutter in customization.
- Standardize interaction patterns across key flows.
